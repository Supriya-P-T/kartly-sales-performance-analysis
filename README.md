# Kartly Sales Performance Analysis and Dashboard Building

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **NextLeap Sales Performance Analysis and Dashboard Building — Milestone 2** case study.

Kartly is a multi-category online marketplace selling products across different categories, brands and geographical locations. The objective of this project is to create a unified view of business performance and support leadership decisions using data-driven analysis.

## Business Problem

Kartly's leadership team faced challenges in understanding its true business performance due to conflicting revenue reports, limited category-level visibility and reliance on overall business totals.

The analysis focuses on understanding revenue contribution, identifying differences across categories and brands, evaluating geographical performance, and examining customer purchasing behaviour.

## Project Objectives

- Define business performance through clear metrics and KPIs.
- Develop analytical questions to guide the investigation.
- Analyse sales performance across categories, brands, cities and customers.
- Identify important differences and opportunities through deeper analysis.
- Build an Excel dashboard for leadership reporting.
- Present findings and recommendations in a concise PDF summary.

## Key Areas of Analysis

- **Category Performance:** Revenue contribution and category-level differences.
- **Brand Performance:** Identify major revenue contributors within categories.
- **Geographical Performance:** Compare cities and states to identify opportunities and underperformance.
- **Customer Behaviour:** Compare repeat and one-time customers and their revenue contribution.
- **Revenue Concentration:** Understand how much the business depends on a limited number of categories, brands or locations.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables and Calculations
- Business Metrics and KPI Analysis
- Dashboard Design
- Data Interpretation and Business Reporting

## Business Value

This project demonstrates how sales data can be structured into meaningful business metrics and translated into insights that support decisions about category investment, brand performance, geographical expansion and customer retention.
